About JLC

Our Story

Hi everyone, nice to meet you! I'm Ryoya Tanabe, the founder and representative of JLC. "TRさん" simply comes from the initials of my name.

I started JLC based on my own experiences living and studying abroad in the UK and China. I know how challenging paperwork, visas, and adapting to a new environment can be, and I was fortunate to receive support along the way.

Later, after marrying internationally and starting a new life with my wife in Japan, I realized how difficult it could be to find clear information and support for foreigners and immigrants here. Even as a Japanese person, I found visa procedures and local government paperwork surprisingly difficult to navigate.

When I visited the Sapporo Regional Immigration Services Bureau, I also saw many people waiting in crowded rooms and hallways, sometimes struggling just to begin their procedures.

That experience made me think: Japan needs a simpler, friendlier way to support people starting a new life here.

That's why I created JLC — to make life in Japan a little easier, clearer, and more welcoming for everyone.
