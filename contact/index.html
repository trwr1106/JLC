Common Questions

Before you reach out, a few quick answers.

Is the community really free?

Yes. Anyone can join our Discord and ask questions at no cost, forever.

How fast is concierge support?

Most requests are scheduled within a few days, sooner for Premium members.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes, cancel anytime before your next billing date — no long-term contract.

Can you help with visas or immigration matters?

We can't file applications or give legal advice on your case — that requires a licensed immigration lawyer (gyoseishoshi). But there's a lot we CAN do:

  • Explain the general process and requirements in plain language
  • Help you organize and prepare your documents
  • Accompany you to your immigration bureau appointment
