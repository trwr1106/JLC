Frequently Asked Questions

Is the community free?

Yes. Joining and participating in the community is free.

Who can join?

Anyone planning to move to Japan, currently living in Japan, international students, foreign residents, and anyone interested in connecting with the community.

Can I ask questions about living in Japan?

Yes. Members can ask about everyday life in Japan, including banking, housing, City Hall procedures, mobile phones, transportation, local recommendations, and more.

Is this only for people living in Sapporo?

No. People from anywhere in Japan are welcome, although we also share information and recommendations related to Sapporo and Hokkaido.

Do you offer personal assistance?

Optional concierge and premium services may be available for people who need more individual support.

How do I join?

Click the Discord button or scan the QR code.
